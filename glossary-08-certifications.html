Certifications & Standards

This section defines 26 certifications, standards and quality marks relevant to UK roofing — from the BBA Certificate and CE Mark to British Standards BS 5534 and BS EN 14782, quality management standards ISO 9001 and ISO 14001, NHBC acceptance, LABC registration, NBS specification tools and EuroCode structural design standards. Metrotile holds the key certifications listed here.

BBA & Agrément Certification

BBA Certificate
BBA Certificate (British Board of Agrément) is the UK's leading independent technical approval for innovative or non-standard construction products. A BBA certificate confirms that a product has been independently tested, assessed and monitored against UK Building Regulations, British Standards and relevant performance criteria. Metrotile holds BBA Certificate 07/4470, covering its range of lightweight steel roofing tiles for pitched roofs. The certificate specifies the approved installation conditions, exposure zone classifications, fixing specifications, minimum pitches, batten grades and structural loading data. BBA certificates are recognised by all UK building control bodies, NHBC, mortgage lenders, architects and specifiers.

See also: Agrément certificate · Metrotile technical documentation

Agrément Certificate
Agrément certificate is the generic term for an independent technical approval certificate for a construction product, named after the French word for "approval". The BBA is the UK's national Agrément body. Agrément certificates are used for products that are outside the scope of established British Standards — including new materials, systems or methods for which no BS test method exists. They provide a route to regulatory compliance for innovative products. Metrotile steel roofing tiles, as a roll-formed steel product with a stone chip finish, are certified under the Agrément route because no single BS covers all aspects of this product type.
UKCAS
UKCAS (United Kingdom Conformity Assessment Scheme) is the UK national accreditation scheme for conformity assessment bodies, operated by UKAS (United Kingdom Accreditation Service). The BBA is accredited by UKAS under UKCAS to perform product certification, including Agrément certification. UKCAS accreditation gives confidence that the BBA's testing and certification procedures meet internationally recognised standards (ISO/IEC 17065 for product certification bodies).
EOTA / ETA
EOTA (European Organisation for Technical Assessment) is the body that co-ordinates European Technical Assessments (ETAs) for construction products across EU member states. An ETA is the European equivalent of a BBA certificate — it certifies a construction product's fitness for its intended use against the EU Construction Products Regulation (CPR). Prior to Brexit, the BBA was a member of EOTA. ETAs are used to obtain the CE Mark for construction products. Post-Brexit, UK products are assessed under the UKCA marking route.

See also: CE Mark · UKCA Mark

British & European Standards

BS 5534
BS 5534:2014+A2:2018 (Slating and Tiling for Pitched Roofs and Vertical Cladding — Code of Practice) is the primary British Standard governing the design and installation of roof tiles in the UK. Key requirements include: specifying tile fixings based on wind uplift calculations; mandatory mechanical fixing for all ridge and hip tiles (eliminating mortar-only bedding for new work); minimum batten grade (C16 or better, free from large knots); breathable underlay specification; and exposure zone classification. All Metrotile installations must comply with BS 5534 to maintain the BBA certification and the 40-year guarantee.

See also: BBA Certificate · Dry-fix

BS 8000-6
BS 8000-6:2013 (Workmanship on Building Sites — Slating and Tiling of Roofs and Cladding) is the workmanship standard that complements BS 5534. While BS 5534 covers design and specification, BS 8000-6 covers the practical standards of workmanship expected of installers on site — including batten fixing, tile laying sequence, ridge and hip installation, flashing work and inspection procedures. Metrotile-approved installers are trained to comply with both BS 5534 and BS 8000-6.
BS EN 14782
BS EN 14782:2006 (Self-supporting Metal Sheet Roofing and Cladding Products — Product Specification and Requirements) is the harmonised European product standard for self-supporting metal sheet roofing products. It specifies the required product characteristics, test methods and performance requirements for metallic roofing sheets including categories for load-bearing capacity, wind uplift resistance, waterproofing and impact resistance. Metrotile steel tiles are tested and declared against BS EN 14782, enabling CE and UKCA marking.

See also: CE Mark

BS EN 12588
BS EN 12588:2006 (Lead and Lead Alloys — Rolled Lead Sheet for Building Purposes — Specifications and Test Methods) is the European standard for lead sheet used in building applications including flashings, soakers, valley linings and weatherings. Lead sheet to BS EN 12588 is classified by code number (Code 3 to Code 8) corresponding to its thickness and weight per m². Code 3 (1.32 mm, 15 kg/m²) is used for soakers and step flashings; Code 4 (1.80 mm, 20 kg/m²) for abutment and valley flashings; Code 5 (2.24 mm, 25 kg/m²) for ridge, parapet and chimney flashings.

See also: Abutment flashing

EuroCode 1 (BS EN 1991)
EuroCode 1 (BS EN 1991) is the European structural design standard for actions on structures — it specifies how to calculate the loads (actions) that structures must be designed to carry. For roofing, the relevant parts are: Part 1-1 (self-weight and imposed loads), Part 1-3 (snow loads) and Part 1-4 (wind actions). Metrotile BBA Certificate 07/4470 references EuroCode 1 Part 1-4 for wind uplift calculations, ensuring that the tile fixing specifications are derived from a consistent and recognised structural design framework.
CE Mark
CE marking indicates that a construction product meets EU harmonised technical specifications (typically a harmonised European product standard such as BS EN 14782) and may be freely marketed across the EU single market. CE marking requires the manufacturer to publish a Declaration of Performance (DoP) listing the product's declared performance characteristics. Metrotile steel tiles carry CE marking under BS EN 14782. Note: from 1 July 2023 (extended deadline), CE marking is being replaced by UKCA marking for products placed on the UK (Great Britain) market, though CE marking remains valid for sales into the EU.

See also: UKCA Mark · BS EN 14782

UKCA Mark
UKCA marking (UK Conformity Assessed) is the UK replacement for CE marking for products placed on the Great Britain market following the UK's exit from the EU. UKCA marking is required for regulated construction products sold in England, Scotland and Wales from the date mandated by the relevant UK statutory instrument. The technical requirements are largely equivalent to the EU harmonised standards used for CE marking. Metrotile's product certification and compliance documentation is maintained in accordance with both CE and UKCA requirements.

Building Regulations

Building Regulations
Building Regulations are the statutory minimum standards for the design and construction of buildings in England and Wales (Scotland has separate Scottish Building Standards; Northern Ireland has separate Building Regulations). They are made under the Building Act 1984 and are administered by building control bodies (local authority or approved inspector). For roofing projects, the relevant Approved Documents are: A (structure), B (fire safety), C (site preparation and resistance to moisture), F (ventilation) and L (conservation of fuel and power). Building Regulations approval is required for new buildings, extensions and certain alterations.

See also: Part A · Part B · Part L

Approved Document A — Structure
Approved Document A (Structure) of the Building Regulations sets out the structural requirements for buildings, including roofs. It references the EuroCodes for structural design (BS EN 1990 series) and requires that structural elements are designed to carry dead, imposed and wind loads with adequate safety margins. For re-roofing projects, Part A requires that any new roof covering does not impose loads in excess of what the existing structure can safely carry. Metrotile's low dead load (7–8 kg/m²) is typically well within the capacity of existing domestic roof structures, avoiding the need for structural strengthening.
Approved Document B — Fire Safety
Approved Document B (Fire Safety) sets requirements for means of escape, internal fire spread (linings), internal fire spread (structure), external fire spread (including the performance of roofing materials), and access for fire services. For roofing, Part B sets the minimum designation (BROOF or equivalent) for roof coverings at specified distances from site boundaries. Metrotile tiles achieve BROOF(t4) classification, meeting the most stringent Part B requirement and allowing installation right up to site boundaries.

See also: BROOF(t4)

Approved Document C — Weathering
Approved Document C (Site Preparation and Resistance to Contaminants and Moisture) includes requirements relating to the resistance of the building fabric to rain penetration and moisture. For roofing, it requires that roof coverings be capable of withstanding the relevant wind-driven rain exposure for the site location without allowing moisture to enter the building. Compliance with BS 5534 and BBA Certificate 07/4470 satisfies Part C requirements for Metrotile roofing systems.
Approved Document L — Thermal
Approved Document L (Conservation of Fuel and Power) sets maximum U-values for new and replacement building elements, including roofs. For new dwellings (Part L1A) the maximum roof U-value is 0.16 W/m²K (target); for existing dwellings being extended or reroofed (Part L1B) it is 0.18 W/m²K. The tiles themselves have negligible thermal resistance; compliance is achieved by specifying an appropriate thickness of insulation below the tiles. Metrotile provides U-value calculation sheets for all standard insulation build-ups to assist with Part L compliance.

Warranty & Industry Bodies

NHBC (National House Building Council)
NHBC is the UK's leading new home warranty and insurance provider, operating the Buildmark warranty scheme which protects new home buyers for 10 years. For a roofing product to be specified on an NHBC-registered new home, it must meet NHBC Technical Standards. Metrotile lightweight steel tiles satisfy NHBC requirements by virtue of their BBA Certificate 07/4470. Builders and developers can specify Metrotile on NHBC-registered projects without any additional NHBC approval process. NHBC technical standards reference BBA certificates as accepted evidence of fitness for purpose.

See also: BBA Certificate

LABC (Local Authority Building Control)
LABC (Local Authority Building Control) is the network of local authority building control teams in England and Wales. LABC also operates a product approval service — LABC Partner Status — which is an additional recognition for products that have been assessed as suitable for use in building control applications. LABC partner status provides local authority building control officers with a quick reference to products that have been pre-assessed for compliance, reducing delays in the approval process. Metrotile has LABC recognition through its BBA Certificate.
Constructionline
Constructionline is the UK's leading procurement and supply chain management service for construction, operated by Capita. Constructionline holds pre-qualification data on thousands of construction companies and product suppliers, enabling clients, specifiers and contractors to verify supplier credentials (financial stability, health & safety, insurance, quality management) without repeating the pre-qualification process for every project. Metrotile is registered with Constructionline, making it straightforward for public sector and framework clients to verify Metrotile's credentials.
ISO 9001
ISO 9001:2015 is the international standard for Quality Management Systems (QMS). Certification to ISO 9001 demonstrates that an organisation has implemented documented processes for quality control, customer focus, continuous improvement and management review, independently audited by a UKAS-accredited certification body. Metrotile holds ISO 9001 certification, providing customers, specifiers and contractors with assurance that its manufacturing and supply processes are consistently managed to meet quality requirements.
ISO 14001
ISO 14001:2015 is the international standard for Environmental Management Systems (EMS). Certification to ISO 14001 demonstrates that an organisation has implemented a systematic approach to managing its environmental impacts — including energy use, waste, emissions and resource consumption — with independent third-party audit. Metrotile holds ISO 14001 certification, supporting its commitment to environmental responsibility across manufacturing, logistics and product lifecycle. ISO 14001 certification is increasingly required in public sector procurement and by clients with corporate sustainability commitments.

See also: ISO 14001 (sustainability)

Specification Tools

NBS
NBS (formerly the National Building Specification, now simply NBS) is the UK's leading construction specification platform, published by RIBA Enterprises. NBS provides structured, clause-based specification language for all aspects of building construction, enabling architects, engineers and specifiers to write technically correct, legally defensible construction specifications. NBS is used on the majority of major UK construction projects. Metrotile is listed in NBS and can be specified directly within NBS-format documents using the NBS Plus product library.

See also: NBS Plus · NBS National BIM Library

→ Metrotile NBS clauses

NBS Plus
NBS Plus is the product data library within the NBS specification platform, providing manufacturer-specific product information, technical data and pre-written NBS clauses that can be inserted into NBS specifications. Being listed on NBS Plus allows a specifier to insert Metrotile-specific product clauses into an NBS specification with a single click, automatically including the correct technical data (BBA certificate number, product reference, fixing specification) in the correct NBS clause format. Metrotile maintains up-to-date NBS Plus listings for all its tile profiles.
NBS National BIM Library
The NBS National BIM Library is a free online library of manufacturer-hosted BIM (Building Information Modelling) objects, providing architects and engineers with 3D models and associated data for construction products in NBS format. BIM objects from the NBS National BIM Library can be imported directly into Autodesk Revit, ArchiCAD and other BIM platforms. Metrotile provides BIM objects for its tile profiles and accessories in the NBS National BIM Library, enabling accurate 3D representation of Metrotile roof systems in BIM models.

See also: BIM

RIBA CPD
RIBA CPD (Royal Institute of British Architects Continuing Professional Development) is the structured learning programme that RIBA members undertake to maintain and develop their professional knowledge. RIBA-registered CPD providers offer seminars, e-learning and site visits that count towards the required annual CPD hours. Metrotile provides RIBA-registered CPD seminars on lightweight steel tile roofing, covering product technology, installation best practice, BBA certification, planning considerations and sustainability credentials — available to architects, structural engineers and roofing specifiers.
